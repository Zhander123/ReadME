
# One Piece

**One Piece** is a popular Japanese anime created by Eiichiro Oda. It follows the adventures of Monkey D. Luffy and his crew as they search for the legendary treasure called One Piece. The anime is full of action, adventure, comedy, and friendship.

## B. Synopsis

One Piece is about a young pirate named Monkey D. Luffy who dreams of becoming the King of the Pirates. When he was young, he ate a Devil Fruit that gave him the ability to stretch his body like rubber. He starts his journey to find the legendary treasure called One Piece.

During his adventure, he meets different people who become his friends and join his crew. They are called the Straw Hat Pirates. Each member has their own dream and goal in life. Together, they travel across different islands and fight powerful enemies. They also experience many challenges, but they never give up on their dreams.

## D. Personal Review

I chose One Piece because it has an interesting story and amazing characters. I enjoy watching the adventures of Luffy and his friends.

### Three Things I Like

1. **Adventure** - Every island has a different story and challenge.
2. **Friendship** - The crew always helps and protects each other.
3. **Emotional Stories** - The anime teaches lessons about dreams, family, and friendship.

> One Piece teaches us to never give up on our dreams and always value our friends.

**My Personal Rating: 10/10**

I recommend One Piece to everyone who enjoys action, adventure, comedy, and friendship. Even though it has many episodes, it is worth watching because of its exciting story.
